The Dormant NATO doctrine

First formulated in a 2023 policy brief and stated fully in “The Best NATO Is a Dormant NATO” (Foreign Affairs, November 2024): the case for burden-shifting and a dormant alliance posture. Its burden-shifting core was incorporated into the 2025 U.S. National Security Strategy.
