About

Dr Sumantra Maitra is a policy scholar working on the impact-heavy problems of statecraft: grand strategy, alliance management, escalation, and the strategic consequences of emerging technology — including frontier AI, drones, and the offense–defense balance.

He is the originator of the “Dormant NATO” doctrine, first formulated in a 2023 policy brief and stated fully in “The Best NATO Is a Dormant NATO” (Foreign Affairs, November 2024). Its burden-shifting core was incorporated into the 2025 U.S. National Security Strategy. His book, Sources of Russian Aggression (Lexington Books, 2024), offers a realist account of when Moscow chooses war.

He was Senior Editor of The American Conservative and Director of Research and Outreach at the American Ideas Institute (2022–2026), and a Senior Fellow at the Center for Renewing America. He is currently an Advisor to the Congressional Greenland Caucus, a Washington Fellow at the Institute for Peace and Diplomacy, and an Elected Fellow (lifetime) of the Royal Historical Society.

He holds a PhD in History and International Relations from the University of Nottingham, a Master of International Studies from the University of Otago, and an MA in Journalism and Mass Communication from Sikkim Manipal University. He is a U.S. legal permanent resident based in Virginia.

He writes the newsletter Clio’s Musings and is on X as @MrMaitra.
